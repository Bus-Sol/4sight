Explore The Functions

Open each section to learn how it works

Customer email and reply detection
Incoming external email sets the opportunity to Customer Waiting. A customer-facing reply changes it to Replied, and the next customer reply returns it to Waiting.
Internal notes and per-user unread counters
The grey Notes badge shows that internal context exists. The teal New Note badge is personal and remains until the current user marks communication read.
Browser and Odoo notifications
Odoo bus notifications surface new customer messages and internal notes in real time. Optional browser alerts can be enabled from the systray, subject to browser permission.
Sales Attention manager view
Filter Customer Waiting, My Unread Messages, My Unread Notes, Activity Overdue, No Next Activity and Needs Sales Attention. Group by salesperson, team, stage or status.
